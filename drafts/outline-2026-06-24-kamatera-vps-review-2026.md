## FAQ Section (5 questions)

**Q1: Is Kamatera a legitimate hosting company?**
A: Yes. Kamatera was founded in 1995 in New York and has been operating continuously for 30+ years. It is one of the longest-running independent cloud infrastructure companies, serving clients in 100+ countries with 14 datacenters.

**Q2: Does Kamatera offer a free trial without a credit card?**
A: Yes. Kamatera offers a 30-day free trial that does not require a credit card. You get full access to cloud servers, firewalls, private networks, and load balancers. Most competitors limit trials to 7-14 days or require a credit card upfront.

**Q3: How does Kamatera's pricing calculator work?**
A: Kamatera does not offer fixed "Starter/Pro/Business" plans. Instead, every server is configured via a pricing calculator where you select CPU type (A/B/D/T), vCPU count, RAM (1GB-512GB), SSD (20GB-1000GB), bandwidth (1TB-10TB+), and OS. The calculator shows the exact monthly cost in real time.

**Q4: Is Kamatera good for EU businesses needing GDPR-compliant hosting?**
A: Yes. Kamatera operates 6 EU datacenters (Amsterdam, Frankfurt, London, Madrid, Paris, Stockholm). EU businesses can host data entirely within EU borders to meet GDPR data residency requirements. The Frankfurt and Amsterdam DCs are the most popular for EU production workloads.

**Q5: Can I run WordPress on Kamatera?**
A: Yes, but you will need to set up the LAMP/LEMP stack manually. Kamatera does not have a one-click WordPress installer or app marketplace. If you want one-click WordPress with auto-updates, see our RackNerd or Hostinger reviews for managed-style options.
