## FAQ
**Q: Does Contabo support IPv6?**
A: Yes, all plans include IPv6 support. However, some advanced networking features are limited compared to premium providers.

**Q: Can I resize my plan without recreating the server?**
A: Yes, but only upward (vertical scaling). Downscaling requires server migration.

**Q: How does Contabo compare to Hetzner for European hosting?**
A: Contabo offers more storage per dollar; Hetzner offers better CPU performance and network speeds. For storage-focused projects, Contabo wins; for compute-heavy workloads, Hetzner is better.

## 结论与推荐 / Conclusion
Contabo excels at one thing: **maximum storage per dollar**. If you need 400GB+ SSD storage on a budget, it's genuinely difficult to beat. However, the outdated control panel and slower support make it less suitable for users who need modern tooling or fast response times. For developers comfortable with self-managed servers and storage-heavy use cases, Contabo is a solid choice — just budget for annual billing to get the best rates.
