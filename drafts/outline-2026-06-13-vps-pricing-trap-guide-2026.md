### H2: FAQ

**Q: What is a VPS pricing bait-and-switch?**
A: A provider advertises a low monthly price (e.g., $2/mo) but only for the first month. After the initial term, the price jumps significantly (e.g., $8/mo). The advertised price is technically available but misleading because most users will pay the higher renewal rate.

**Q: Does RackNerd have hidden renewal fees?**
A: No. RackNerd's annual deals are billed annually at a fixed rate. There is no month-to-month cycle with a hidden higher rate. When the year ends, users can renew at a similar promotional rate or let the service expire. Community feedback confirms transparent billing.

**Q: Which VPS provider has the most transparent pricing?**
A: InterServer has the strongest price lock guarantee — $6/mo forever. Vultr and DigitalOcean have flat hourly pricing with no intro/renewal distinction. RackNerd offers the best value for users willing to commit to annual deals.

**Q: How can I avoid VPS pricing traps?**
A: (1) Always look for the renewal price — not just the intro price. (2) Read LowEndTalk or LowEndBox reviews. (3) Calculate 12-month total cost using the formula above. (4) Choose providers with flat pricing (Vultr, DO, InterServer) or proven transparent renewal patterns (RackNerd).

**Q: Are there any "too good to be true" signs?**
A: Yes — if the monthly price is wildly below market rate for the specs offered ($2/mo for a 2GB Ryzen 9 VPS), there's almost always a catch. Check the provider's checkout page for fine print about renewal pricing. If it's not listed, ask in the community before buying.

**Q: Is Contabo's renewal pricing fair?**
A: Contabo has moderate renewal increases. While not as dramatic as the Godlike.Host case, users should factor in a 1.5-2x renewal markup when calculating annual cost. For storage-heavy workloads, the raw capacity may still justify the renewal price.
