## Editorial Differentiation
- **Rare geographic footprint** — New Zealand, South Africa, Chile. These are NOT typical VPS markets where hyperscalers compete, and Zappiehost is one of the few Western-managed providers with self-owned AS in all three regions.
- **Free BGP session** on Premium KVM — uncommon in the budget tier; useful for operators with their own IP allocations.
- **Multi-RIR membership** (ARIN + RIPE + APNIC + Afrinic) — unusual breadth for a small operator.
- **Bitcoin/crypto payment** — explicit privacy-first positioning.
- **Self-managed only** — buyer handles Linux administration; no managed tier.
- **Semi-annual pricing model** — entry $6/mo is higher than RackNerd annual-special floor; buyers paying monthly will pay more.
- Strong for: APAC + LATAM + Africa buyers who need a Western-managed VPS in rare locations; operators needing free BGP; crypto-payments buyers.
- Weak for: budget-focused buyers who want the absolute cheapest VPS (RackNerd/CrownCloud undercut on price); managed services buyers; GPU/CPU-intensive workloads (only 6 cores on top tier); buyers needing low-latency US/EU coverage (no US/EU DCs).
