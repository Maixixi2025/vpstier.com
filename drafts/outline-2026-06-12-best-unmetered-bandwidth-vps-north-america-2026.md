### H2: FAQ

**Q: What does "unmetered bandwidth" actually mean?**
A: Unmetered bandwidth means the provider does not enforce a hard monthly data transfer cap. Most providers apply a Fair Use Policy or port-speed throttling to prevent abuse. True unmetered providers like BuyVM enforce no cap and no throttle.

**Q: Is RackNerd bandwidth truly unmetered?**

**Q: Can I run a streaming service on an unmetered VPS?**
A: Yes, but check the provider's Terms of Service. Most budget-friendly unmetered VPS plans have media-specific restrictions. RackNerd allows media streaming on standard plans, making it a top choice for streaming use cases.

**Q: Do I need unmetered bandwidth for AI workloads?**
A: Only if you serve large model outputs or media over a network. For training and inference where data stays local, a standard metered VPS is sufficient. For agents with heavy network I/O, unmetered plans add peace of mind.
